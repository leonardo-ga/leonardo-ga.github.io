Research interests

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Università degli Studi di Milano, supervised by Lorenzo Luperi Baglini, with co-advisor Luca Reggio.

My interests lie in mathematical logic, particularly nonstandard analysis and ultrafilters. My work concerns extensions of relations to spaces of ultrafilters. I am also interested in model theory, set theory, category theory, and connections with Ramsey theory.
